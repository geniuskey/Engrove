## Keep data and execution together

In a typical engineering team, test data lives in spreadsheets, source files on a NAS, dates in calendars, and
follow-up work in an issue tracker. Engrove connects these systems around **engineering context and traceability**
instead of forcing every source into one storage product.

<div class="product-flow">
  <div><strong>01 · Model</strong><span>Define samples, equipment, and test subjects in the team's language.</span></div>
  <div><strong>02 · Measure</strong><span>Record results while preserving physical units and source evidence.</span></div>
  <div><strong>03 · Evaluate</strong><span>Evaluate results against an immutable specification revision.</span></div>
  <div><strong>04 · Act</strong><span>Create follow-up work that retains the exact evidence.</span></div>
  <div><strong>05 · Review</strong><span>Reconstruct decisions through dashboards, reviews, and audit history.</span></div>
</div>
